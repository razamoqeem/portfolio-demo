AMK Digital Agency

We are an AI-based digital marketing and search engine optimization agency. We build SEO systems that work with generative search engines, large language model crawlers and traditional search indexes.

Our vision

To lead the next stage of search experience optimization and generative engine optimization. We see a future where digital authority is not bought, but built through clear credibility signals, semantic alignment and transparency.

Our purpose

To help close the digital trust gap. Searchers and AI systems look for verification. We build data layers, schema structures and E-E-A-T strategies that connect first impressions to real enquiries.

Our goals

To replace guesswork with a clear framework: sound content structure, clean technical crawling, better search index presence and support for sustainable growth.

How we work

Generative engine optimization

We structure pages so that AI search systems such as Google Gemini, Perplexity and ChatGPT search can find, understand and cite them.

Technical crawl checks

We run diagnostic scans covering index status, Core Web Vitals and crawl settings so search engines can reach key content.

Credibility and E-E-A-T

We align site structure with Google's experience, expertise, authoritativeness and trust guidance, using structured data, author details and clear trust signals.
